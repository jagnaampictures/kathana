## 1. Welcome to Kathana Support

Kathana Beta is an early public release.

Support exists to help users with:

- installation;
- application problems;
- account access;
- email verification;
- Beta entitlement;
- trial issues;
- saving and opening projects;
- import and export problems;
- application behaviour;
- security concerns;
- and general questions about the Beta.

We aim to provide useful, practical assistance while protecting the privacy of your creative work.

---

# 2. Most Important Rule: Protect Your Screenplay

### Please do not send your screenplay unless it is genuinely necessary.

Kathana is designed so that screenplay files remain local to your computer.

For most support requests, we do **not** need your screenplay.

Please avoid attaching:

- `.kathana` files;
- screenplay PDFs;
- DOCX screenplay files;
- Fountain screenplay files;
- screenplay screenshots containing confidential material;
- unpublished story material;
- private production documents;
- or other sensitive creative work.

If the problem can be demonstrated without your screenplay, please use a **test project or minimal example** instead.

---

# 3. What to Include in a Support Request

A useful support request normally includes:

### Application

- Kathana version
- Operating system
- Computer architecture where relevant

### Problem

- What you were trying to do
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Whether the problem happens every time
- Whether the problem started after an update

### Evidence

Where useful, include:

- screenshots;
- exact error messages;
- short screen recordings;
- relevant application information;
- or steps that reproduce the problem.

Please remove private or confidential information before sending screenshots or recordings.

---

# 4. Suggested Bug Report Format

You can use the following structure:

**Subject:** Kathana Beta — Bug Report

**Kathana version:**  
Kathana Beta v0.1.0

**Operating system:**  
Example: macOS

**What I was doing:**  
Describe the action.

**Expected result:**  
Describe what should have happened.

**Actual result:**  
Describe what happened instead.

**Steps to reproduce:**

1. Step one
2. Step two
3. Step three

**Does it happen every time?**  
Yes / No

**Additional information:**  
Any other useful details.

---

# 5. Account and Sign-In Problems

If you cannot sign in, tell us:

- the email address associated with the account;
- the Kathana version;
- what happens after entering your email;
- whether the verification email arrives;
- whether the verification code is accepted;
- and any error message shown.

### Never send your 6-digit verification code.

Support does not need your current authentication code.

If you accidentally disclose a verification code, contact support immediately and consider securing your email account.

---

# 6. Verification Email Problems

If the 6-digit verification email does not arrive:

1. Check the email address you entered.
2. Check Spam or Junk folders.
3. Wait briefly before requesting another code.
4. Try requesting a new code.
5. Make sure you are using the most recently issued code.
6. If the problem continues, contact support.

Include:

- the email address used for Kathana;
- approximate time of the request;
- Kathana version;
- operating system;
- and what happened after requesting the code.

Do not send the verification code itself.

---

# 7. Trial Problems

If your 14-day trial behaves unexpectedly, provide:

- Kathana version;
- operating system;
- approximate date you first started the trial;
- what the application currently displays;
- and what you expected to happen.

Do not send your screenplay to demonstrate a trial problem.

A support investigation can normally be performed using a test project.

---

# 8. Trial Expiry

When the 14-day trial ends, Kathana may require an eligible account sign-in to continue editing.

Existing local screenplay projects are not intended to be deleted because the trial ends.

Depending on the application state, existing projects may remain available for:

- opening;
- reading;
- and exporting.

Editing may require an eligible account.

If this behaviour does not match what you observe, contact support.

---

# 9. Saving Problems

If Kathana does not appear to save correctly, provide:

- Kathana version;
- operating system;
- whether the project is a `.kathana` file;
- where you attempted to save it;
- what happened when you selected Save or Save As;
- and any error message.

### Do not immediately overwrite your only copy.

If you believe a project is at risk:

1. Stop making unnecessary changes.
2. Preserve the original file.
3. Preserve any available backup.
4. Avoid repeatedly opening and saving a potentially damaged file.
5. Contact support with a description of the problem.

---

# 10. `.kathana` File Problems

If a `.kathana` file does not open:

Please provide:

- Kathana version;
- operating system;
- what happens when you attempt to open the file;
- whether the file previously opened successfully;
- whether the problem started after a particular action;
- and any error message.

### Do not modify the internal contents of the `.kathana` file unless instructed by someone who understands the format.

Keep the original file unchanged.

If you have a backup copy, preserve that copy separately.

---

# 11. Damaged or Corrupted Projects

If you believe a screenplay project has become damaged:

**Do not immediately overwrite the original file.**

Keep:

- the original `.kathana` file;
- any backup copy;
- any automatically created recovery copy;
- and information about when the problem first appeared.

Contact support before repeatedly attempting repairs.

### Important

Kathana support cannot guarantee recovery of a damaged or deleted screenplay.

Because screenplay files are intentionally kept locally, users remain responsible for maintaining independent backups.

---

# 12. Import Problems

For DOCX or other supported imports, tell us:

- source file type;
- Kathana version;
- operating system;
- what imported correctly;
- what did not;
- and whether the problem can be reproduced with another file.

Do not send an unpublished screenplay if the problem can be demonstrated with a simple test document.

If you must provide a file for investigation, remove confidential material first where possible.

---

# 13. Export Problems

If Word, PDF, or Fountain export does not behave as expected, provide:

- Kathana version;
- operating system;
- export format;
- what you expected;
- what happened;
- and screenshots of the relevant result if useful.

Before sending an exported file to support, remove confidential material where possible.

---

# 14. Kannada and Indian-Script Problems

If you experience a text-handling problem involving Kannada or another Indian script, please include:

- Kathana version;
- operating system;
- script/language being used;
- the action that caused the problem;
- what happened;
- and whether the problem occurs in a new test project.

Where possible, demonstrate the problem using a small test phrase rather than an entire screenplay.

This helps us investigate text-handling issues without requiring access to your creative work.

---

# 15. Font and Rendering Problems

If text appears incorrectly:

Tell us:

- the script/language;
- the font selected;
- where the problem appears;
- whether it occurs in the editor, preview, or export;
- and the operating system.

If the problem involves a third-party font installed on your computer, mention the font name and version where known.

Do not send copyrighted or licensed font files to support unless specifically requested and legally appropriate.

---

# 16. Multiple-Window Problems

If a problem occurs while working with multiple Kathana windows, tell us:

- how many windows were open;
- which project each window contained;
- what action you performed;
- whether both windows were editing the same file;
- and what happened.

For testing, use a copy or test project whenever possible.

---

# 17. Performance Problems

If Kathana becomes slow, freezes, or behaves unexpectedly, provide:

- Kathana version;
- operating system;
- computer model where useful;
- approximate project size;
- what you were doing when the problem occurred;
- whether the issue happens with a new blank project;
- and whether restarting Kathana changes the behaviour.

Do not send the screenplay itself unless it is genuinely necessary.

---

# 18. Crash Reports

If Kathana crashes:

Tell us:

- what action you were performing;
- whether the crash is repeatable;
- whether it occurs with a new project;
- operating system;
- Kathana version;
- and any crash message available.

If the operating system provides a crash report, you may include the relevant diagnostic information after reviewing it for private information.

Do not include your screenplay merely because a crash occurred.

---

# 19. Security Reports

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability, contact:

**jagnaam.kathana.app@gmail.com**

Please provide:

- a description of the issue;
- affected Kathana version;
- steps to reproduce;
- expected security impact;
- and supporting evidence where appropriate.

Please avoid publicly publishing sensitive vulnerability details before giving us a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

Do not attempt to access another person's account, files, or private information while investigating a suspected vulnerability.

# 21. Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Kathana account, email:

**jagnaam.kathana.app@gmail.com**

Please contact us from the email address associated with the relevant account where possible.

Deleting an account does **not** automatically delete `.kathana` files or other screenplay files stored on your computer.

You remain responsible for managing local files.

Some information may need to be retained where required by applicable law or reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution, or legitimate record-keeping.

# 22. Feature Requests

Kathana is actively developing during the Beta period.

If you have a feature request, tell us:

- what you want to accomplish;
- why it would help your writing;
- how you currently work around the problem;
- and, where useful, an example workflow.

A feature request does not guarantee that the feature will be implemented.

# 23. Community

Kathana may provide community resources for discussion and knowledge sharing.

Community services are separate from Kathana's local screenplay storage.

When participating:

- do not post private screenplay material;
- do not post confidential production information;
- do not publish another person's personal information;
- do not share authentication codes;
- and follow the applicable rules of the community platform.

# 24. What Support Can and Cannot Do

### Support can help with:

- installation questions;
- account access;
- verification problems;
- Beta entitlement;
- trial issues;
- application errors;
- supported file workflows;
- import/export issues;
- documentation;
- and reproducible bugs.

### Support cannot guarantee:

- recovery of deleted files;
- recovery of corrupted files;
- restoration of overwritten work;
- recovery of files from failed storage devices;
- compatibility with every third-party application;
- preservation of every formatting characteristic during conversion;
- or uninterrupted Beta availability.

---

# 25. Protecting Your Creative Work During Support

Before sending anything to support:

### Ask yourself:

**“Does support actually need to see my screenplay?”**

If the answer is no, do not send it.

Prefer:

- test projects;
- minimal examples;
- cropped screenshots;
- short test text;
- and descriptions of the problem.

If you voluntarily send a screenplay or other creative work, you should understand that it becomes part of the support communication and may need to be processed to investigate your request.

Only send material you are comfortable providing for that purpose.

---

# 26. Support Response Times

Kathana Beta support is provided on a reasonable-efforts basis.

Response times may vary depending on:

- issue severity;
- complexity;
- available information;
- Beta workload;
- and whether the problem can be reproduced.

A support request does not guarantee an immediate response.
