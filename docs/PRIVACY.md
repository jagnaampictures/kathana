## 1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how JAGNAAM PICTURES (“JAGNAAM PICTURES”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with Kathana Beta v0.1.0 (“Kathana”, “the Application”, or “the Service”).

Kathana is designed as an **offline-first, local-first screenwriting application**.

A fundamental design principle of Kathana is:

> **Download is public. Trial is public. Account is identity. Screenplays remain local.**

Kathana is designed so that a writer's screenplay files are created, opened, edited, saved, and exported on the writer's own device.

This Privacy Policy therefore distinguishes carefully between:

1. **Account and service information**, which may be processed by our account infrastructure; and
2. **Screenplay and creative content**, which Kathana is designed not to upload to our servers as part of its normal operation.

## 5. Your Screenplays and Creative Work

### 5.1 Screenplays Remain on Your Device

Kathana is designed around local screenplay storage.

Your `.kathana` files are intended to remain on the device where you create or save them.

Kathana's account system is **not intended to store your screenplay content**.

We do not operate Kathana Beta as a cloud screenplay-storage or screenplay-synchronisation service.

### 5.2 No Routine Screenplay Upload

Kathana does not require you to upload your screenplay to our servers in order to:

- write;
- save;
- open;
- edit;
- preview;
- import supported files; or
- export your work.

Your normal writing activity therefore does not require sending screenplay content to our account backend.

### 5.3 No Cloud Synchronisation

Kathana Beta does not provide cloud screenplay synchronisation.

If you use Kathana on more than one computer, a screenplay saved on one computer does not automatically become available on another computer through your Kathana account.

### 5.4 Backups

Kathana may provide local backup and recovery features.

These features operate on the user's device or on storage locations selected by the user.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of important creative work.

We do not represent the Kathana account service as a replacement for your own backup strategy.

---

## 6. The Kathana Trial

Kathana Beta includes a 14-day full-feature trial.

The trial state is designed to be maintained locally on the user's device.

The trial mechanism is not intended to transmit your screenplay content to our servers.

After the applicable trial period ends, Kathana may require an eligible account sign-in to continue editing.

Existing local screenplay files remain local.

---

## 7. What Happens After Trial Expiry

After trial expiry, Kathana may allow you to:

- open existing local screenplay files;
- read your existing work; and
- export your existing work.

Editing may require an eligible Kathana account.

This access model does not transfer ownership of your screenplay to JAGNAAM PICTURES and does not require your screenplay to be uploaded to our servers.

## 12. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect Through Normal Writing

Kathana Beta is not designed to routinely collect or upload:

- the text of your screenplay;
- your scene contents;
- your dialogue;
- your screenplay notes;
- your character information;
- your screenplay structure;
- your local `.kathana` files;
- your local backup files.

The absence of routine cloud storage does not mean that information can never leave your device under every circumstance.

For example, information may be shared if you voluntarily attach a file to a support request, use a third-party service, or otherwise choose to transmit information yourself.

## 13. Diagnostics and Technical Information

Kathana may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, or improve the application and account service.

Where diagnostic information is collected, the goal is to avoid collecting screenplay content unless such content is explicitly and voluntarily provided by you.

Kathana Beta is not intended to operate as a behavioural surveillance system or as a service for analysing the contents of writers' screenplays.

The architecture intentionally separates:

**Account identity**

from

This separation is an important part of Kathana's privacy design.
